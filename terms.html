Terms

Reck Profile Backup is a privately operated utility for its owner's personal Windows files. This website supplies application information; it does not offer a public hosted backup service or account registration.

Authorized use

Use the application only with files, storage accounts and drives you own or are authorized to use. Approve Google or Microsoft connections only for the intended account, and review the configured source selection and exclusions.

Operating responsibilities

Keep the archive password separately, maintain sufficient local, USB and cloud space, keep the computer available for scheduled operations, inspect reported failures and verify recovery by restoring sample files. Provider subscriptions and storage limits are separate from the application.

Limitations

Backups may be incomplete when files are busy, inaccessible, excluded or changed during copying. The application does not create a complete Windows system image and does not restore every form of Windows filesystem metadata. It cannot guarantee protection against all hardware, software, security or account failures.

Privacy and contact

The application's data practices are described in its privacy policy. Contact its owner through the support email configured on the Google OAuth consent screen.
